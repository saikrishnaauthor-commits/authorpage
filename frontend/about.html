Sai Krishna Seelaboyina

Author & Electrical Engineer

Meet the Author

Sai Krishna Seelaboyina is an electrical engineer and author whose writing bridges the worlds of analytical precision and deep human emotion.

Education & Professional Journey

An alumnus of the prestigious NIT Warangal, Sai Krishna applies his technical background and structured thinking to both his engineering career and his writing. Professionally, he serves as an Electrical Engineer with APTRANSCO, having previously contributed his expertise as Engineer. His professional experiences within large-scale utility and planning systems have deeply influenced his understanding of institutional frameworks, societal pressures, and human resilience.

Literary Portfolio

Sai Krishna made a striking authorial debut with KaalYug: Shadows of Dark Web, a high-stakes techno-thriller that explores cybercrime, digital-age governance, and the modern battle between order and chaos. Demonstrating versatility across genres, he also self-published Pages of Us (subtitled Where Love Ends, Life Begins), an intimate, soul-touching drama exploring love, loss, and the quiet complexities of human relationships. He further expanded his thrilling narrative universes with The Indestructible Kali, continuing his exploration of ethics, technology, and justice. His upcoming works include From the Passenger Seat.

Creative Philosophy & Personal Interests

Drawing inspiration from structural challenges, unspoken emotions, and the quiet resilience found in everyday people, his stories examine the intersection of personal morality and modern realities. For Sai Krishna, stories are not merely meant to be told, but deeply felt. Outside of his engineering career and writing desk, he channels his creativity into photography and poetic reflections, sharing his artistic perspective with readers through his Instagram.
