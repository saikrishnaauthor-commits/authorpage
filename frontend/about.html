Meet the author

Sai Krishna Seelaboyina is an electrical engineer and author whose writing bridges the worlds of analytical precision and deep human emotion.

Education & professional journey

An alumnus of NIT Warangal, Sai Krishna applies his technical background and structured thinking to both his engineering career and his writing. He serves as an Electrical Engineer with APTRANSCO. His experience within large-scale utility and planning systems has deeply influenced his understanding of institutional frameworks, societal pressures and human resilience.

Literary portfolio

Sai Krishna made his debut with KaalYug: Shadows of Dark Web, a high-stakes techno-thriller exploring cybercrime, digital-age governance and the modern battle between order and chaos. Showing his range, he also self-published Pages of Us (Where Love Ends, Life Begins), an intimate drama about love, loss and the quiet complexities of human relationships. He expanded his narrative universe with The Indestructible Kali, continuing his exploration of ethics, technology and justice.

Creative philosophy & interests

Drawing inspiration from structural challenges, unspoken emotions and the quiet resilience found in everyday people, his stories examine the intersection of personal morality and modern realities. Outside engineering and writing, he channels his creativity into photography and poetic reflections, which he shares with readers on Instagram.

For Sai Krishna, stories are not merely meant to be told, but deeply felt.
